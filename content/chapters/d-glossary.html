The formulas behind the entries

Several entries rest on a formula. Each one is below with an example you can check by hand. All of it is arithmetic, with a $100 pot and a $50 bet as the running case.

Counts of hands also anchor many entries. The deck has 52 x 51 / 2 = 1,326 two-card starting hands, which fall into 169 hand types. A combo is one of those 1,326.
