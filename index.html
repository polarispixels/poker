Where every hand begins

Before the first card is dealt, the most important decision is which hands to play at all. David Sklansky and Mason Malmuth ranked the 169 starting hands of Texas Hold'em into eight groups. Group 1 is the five hands you raise from anywhere: aces, kings, queens, jacks, and ace-king suited.

AKs, Group 1

The groups are a starting point, not a rulebook. Good players widen them as they move toward the button, and tighten them when the table is full and the action is loose. Most leaks come from playing the weakest groups out of position.

What Doyle Brunson might say

He would push back on folding so much. A tight range keeps you safe, but the player who applies pressure controls the hand. Pick your spots, then make the other player decide.
