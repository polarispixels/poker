The Long Game Docs / Figures

Figures

Figures are first-class parts of this book. This page shows every figure type and page component with the source that makes it. A chapter is written by copying these and changing the content.

Tones name a meaning, not a color: ink is the main line or the hero, accent (oxblood) is risk, loss, a bluff, or the villain, accent2 (teal) is value or the alternative, gold is a highlight, and muted and paper are structure. Soft versions exist: ink-soft, accent-soft, accent2-soft, gold-soft. Every kit figure needs a label: a sentence for readers who cannot see it.

Cards (shorthand)

Write cards anywhere in text as [[As Kh]]. Suits are s, h, d, c. Use x for any suit and ?? for a face-down card. The build expands them, so they need no script.

Hero holds A♠K♠ on a flop of T♠9♠2♦. The villain shows Q♥Q♦. A hand like A7 has no suits named, and is unknown.

<p>Hero holds [[As Ks]] on a flop of [[Ts 9s 2d]]. The villain shows [[Qh Qd]]. A hand like [[Ax 7x]] has no suits named, and [[?? ??]] is unknown.</p>

table

A poker table seen from above. Seats run clockwise in the order given, and the hero sits at the bottom. Each seat has "pos" and may have "stack", "bet", "cards", "note", and "state" (hero, villain, active, fold). "button" is the index of the dealer seat. "pot", "board", and "center" go in the middle.

Drill

A question with its answer folded away. Use several in a row for practice.

The pot is $80 and your opponent bets $40. What equity do you need to call?

40 / (80 + 40 + 40) = 40 / 160 = 25%.

Table Math

Every chapter ends with exactly one. One shortcut, a worked example, and how far off the shortcut can be.

Table Math

The rule of 2 and 4

The shortcut. Multiply your outs by 4 on the flop, or by 2 on the turn.

Flush draw on the flop: 9 x 4 = 36% (exact: 35%)

Where it bends. Above 8 outs on the flop the rule runs high. Fifteen outs gives 60%; the exact figure is 54%.
